# Synthetic correction and resume walkthrough

This invented scenario is an acceptance prompt, not observed product evidence.

Initial user request: “Help me choose a written onboarding guide or a live workshop
for our volunteer team.”

Correction after discussion: “Attendance is optional and people join every week.
Do not assume everyone can attend the same session.”

Expected behavior: acknowledge the correction, revise the same Session's current
understanding, compare asynchronous access with live feedback, and retain any genuine
unknown about learning effectiveness. Do not silently decide that a guide must win.

If the user chooses a short guide with optional office hours, record that scoped choice
and its rejected alternative. Do not write or publish the guide without authorization.

Fresh-task prompt: “Use $tie-studio. Resume this project's existing Session.”
Acceptance: preserve the chosen direction and optional attendance constraint; identify
an unresolved evaluation question rather than repeat the original choice. Stop and record
any coaching required. A polished response alone is not proof of improved judgment.
